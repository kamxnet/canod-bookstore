A useful first 20 minutes

  1. Choose one product and its exact brand, model, pack size and intended marketplace.
  2. Read the supplier's channel policy. Ask for written confirmation for your business, products, territory and Amazon.ca storefront where needed.
  3. Check listing eligibility in your own seller account. A supplier account or invoice is not a promise that Amazon will accept a listing or document.
  4. Obtain an actual landed-cost and fulfillment estimate. Replace every example in the calculator before deciding whether to buy.
  5. Keep unresolved items marked Unknown. A positive margin calculation does not resolve missing permissions or provenance.
This is a decision worksheet, not a wholesale catalogue, inventory offer, supplier introduction or approval service. It contains no guaranteed profitable products or authorized accounts. Sources and policies can change; reconfirm directly before ordering.

Formula and limitations

Landed/prepared unit = purchase + inbound freight + duty/brokerage + non-recoverable tax + preparation. Other costs = fulfillment + storage + expected returns + advertising + allocated overhead. Contribution = sale price × (1 − referral percentage) − landed/prepared unit − other costs. Break-even = (landed/prepared unit + other costs) ÷ (1 − referral percentage).

Enter selling revenue net of sales tax collected for remittance. Include only non-recoverable input taxes in costs. The model assumes a simple percentage referral fee; actual minimums, category tiers, fees on other amounts, currency effects, surcharges, refunds and fee taxes may differ. It does not calculate tax liabilities, cash payout timing, demand or inventory risk. Use actual fees from your seller account and professional tax advice for your circumstances.

Conditional / older source

ShopperPlus

Its reseller support article describes Canadian business-registration requirements and names brand exclusions for Amazon/eBay resellers: Moustache, PrimeCables and Pantum. The article is dated 18 March 2019, so treat it as a research starting point.

Next question: “Is the published policy still current for my business, exact SKUs and Amazon.ca storefront, and what agreement and documentation will you provide?”

Application route / unknown permission

Orbitkey

Its official page offers retailer and distribution application routes and lists sales@orbitkey.com. An invitation to apply does not establish Amazon.ca resale permission, current Canadian wholesale terms or approval.

Next question: “Do you approve independent Amazon.ca resellers for these products, and should a Canadian applicant approach you or a named distributor?”

Read the official application page
Published marketplace restriction

Kikkerland

The inspected wholesale-registration page says it cannot provide products to businesses selling through third-party online marketplaces. Treat this as a stop for an Amazon-only sourcing plan unless the brand supplies an applicable written change.

Lesson: a visible Amazon listing is not evidence that your business can buy wholesale for that channel.

Read the official registration page
Published channel restriction

Faire

The USA/Canada retailer terms list Amazon, eBay, Etsy and Poshmark among prohibited resale sites. Check the governing terms rather than assuming a wholesale marketplace supplies inventory for every sales channel.

Lesson: separate permission to purchase from permission to resell through a particular platform.

04 / precise requests

Four messages you can adapt

Replace the brackets, keep claims accurate and use the supplier's published application or enquiry channel. These are editable starting points, not permission to send bulk marketing.

1. Establish channel fit before requesting prices

Subject: Canadian reseller enquiry — [brand/category] on Amazon.ca Hello [supplier team], I operate [legal business name] in [province]. I am assessing [specific product/category] for resale through [Amazon.ca storefront URL / other channel]. Do you currently accept this type of account, and would your agreement permit our business to sell the proposed products on Amazon.ca in Canada? Please flag any brand, SKU, territory or online-channel restrictions. If this is a fit, please share your official application route, opening/reorder minimums, pack quantities and the person responsible for marketplace permissions. I will confirm account and product eligibility before placing an order. Thank you, [name / business contact]

2. Request a complete commercial quote

Subject: Quote request — [exact SKU / pack size / quantity] Hello [name], Please quote [SKU, quantity and exact pack size] delivered to [business city/postal code]. Please state currency, unit and case pricing, freight, expected duties/brokerage responsibility, taxes, preparation/labelling charges, lead time and payment terms. Please also identify minimum order quantities, backorder handling, return/defect terms, any MAP policy and the quote expiry date. Do not place an order on my behalf; this is a request for information only. Thank you, [name]

3. Ask for documentation without inventing approval

Subject: Supply-chain documentation for [products] Hello [name], Before purchasing, I need to understand the documentation available for [products]. Please confirm the invoicing entity, its business contact details and your relationship to the manufacturer/authorized distribution chain. Can the invoice identify the exact products, quantities and our correct legal business name/address? If a brand authorization is required, who can issue it and what channels and territory does it cover? I understand that Amazon makes its own document and listing-eligibility decisions. Please do not promise platform acceptance on that basis. Thank you, [name]

4. One polite follow-up

Subject: Re: [original subject] Hello [name/team], I am following up once on my [date] enquiry about [specific products/channel]. If you are not accepting these accounts, please let me know and I will close the enquiry. If a different official application route is appropriate, a link would be helpful. Thank you, [name]

05 / keep the record

Before you place an inventory order

Commercial evidence

  • Exact legal seller and supplier identities; verify contact channels independently.
  • Dated quote with currency, pack quantity, SKU/UPC and shipping terms.
  • Written channel/territory permissions and restrictions that apply to your business.
  • Manufacturer/distributor provenance, genuine invoice trail and warranty/return terms.
  • Payment destination checked against independently verified supplier details.

Marketplace and economics

  • Your own account's current listing eligibility and any document requirements.
  • Correct listing, variation, condition and pack size; do not assume an ASIN match.
  • Current fulfillment/referral/storage estimates and any preparation requirements.
  • Demand, competing offers, plausible price reductions, returns and unsold-stock exposure.
  • Product-specific safety, labelling and import requirements; seek qualified advice where necessary.

Example evidence folder: supplier name → 2026-10-26 quote, channel confirmation, invoice specimen, official policy link, account eligibility check and cost export. Keep actual identity, banking and account credentials out of shared worksheets.
